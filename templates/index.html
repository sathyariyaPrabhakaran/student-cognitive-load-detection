Research safeguards

The implementation uses a real public dataset, preserves target semantics, avoids synthetic labels, uses participant-level holdout testing, and reports actual metrics generated by the training run. Workload-state output is treated as an operational cognitive-load proxy rather than a clinical diagnosis.
