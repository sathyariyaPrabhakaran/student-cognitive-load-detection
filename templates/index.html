What makes this final-year level

Reproducible evaluationStratified 5-fold cross-validation, held-out testing, macro precision/recall/F1 and confusion matrices.
Feature engineeringTransforms raw interaction signals into fatigue, engagement, activity, idle and interaction-rate proxies.
Model selectionRandom Forest baseline is compared against HistGradientBoosting and RBF-SVM instead of reporting a single arbitrary classifier.
Responsible interpretationPredictions are treated as cognitive-load estimates from behavioural patterns, not clinical or psychological diagnoses.
